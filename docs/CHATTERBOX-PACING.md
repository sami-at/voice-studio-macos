# Chatterbox Energetic Explainer pacing — Stage A (investigation) and Stage B (implemented)

25 October 2026. Stage A changed nothing; Stage B (below) implements your choice. All Stage A variants were built from your saved Chatterbox takes with Voice Studio's own processing and assembly; no model was run. Listen at `samples/pacing/index.html` and pick by ear.

## Compared recordings

| | Session | Engine | Length |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Qwen3 | `a617e891` | Qwen3 | 37.9 s |
| Chatterbox | `c40802da` | Chatterbox (exaggeration 0.5) | 47.0 s |

Both are Hal, Energetic Explainer, with the same script, the same 10 sections and 127 words. Settings: reference pace 1.15×, final speed 1.04×, paragraph pause 0.34 s, section pause 0.18 s.

## How Energetic Explainer reaches Chatterbox today

| Setting | Effect on Chatterbox |
|---|---|
| **Reference pace 1.15×** | Stretches the reference clip Chatterbox listens to. Its effect on Chatterbox's delivery is unmeasured; it was measured only for Qwen3, where it speeds delivery up. |
| **Final speed 1.04×** | The pitch-preserving stretch after generation; identical for both engines. |
| **Chatterbox `speed`** | Ignored by the installed model ("Chatterbox doesn't support speed adjustment"). |
| **`cfg_weight`** | Voice Studio uses **0.0**; Chatterbox's own default is 0.5. Above 0 it runs a second forward pass every step, so generation is roughly 2× slower. Chatterbox's guidance says this setting affects pacing. **Untested here.** It is the only generation-level lever, and testing it needs new generation. |
| **Pauses** | Style gaps (0.34 s between paragraphs) are added only to make up whatever quiet each take already ends with. The app never deletes a take's own quiet. |

## Where the 9.1 seconds go

| | Qwen3 | Chatterbox | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Voiced speech | 34.1 s (3.73 words/s) | 37.1 s (3.42 words/s) | +3.1 s (34 %) |
| Pauses inside sections (mostly commas, 0.26–0.30 s each) | 0.1 s | 1.8 s | +1.8 s (19 %) |
| Silence between sections | 3.3 s (≈ 0.37 s per gap) | 7.3 s (≈ 0.81 s per gap) | **+4.0 s (43 %)** |
| Opening and closing | 0.5 s | 0.8 s | +0.3 s |

**Why Chatterbox's gaps are long.** After each take's last word:
1. the audible decay lasts only **20–110 ms**;
2. then come **300–500 ms of near-silence at about −90 dB**, generated by the model.

The app keeps all of that, so every gap ends up near 0.8 s. Section 9 is the exception: a faint sound continues for 610 ms, and it is kept.

**Chatterbox speaks only about 9 % slower.** It pauses at commas, where Qwen3 rushes through, which may be part of why it sounds more natural.

## Variants (same takes, no new generation)

| Version | Total | Speaking rate | Typical gap | Words | Cut endings | Periodicity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Qwen3 original | 37.9 s | 3.73 | 0.36 s | ok | — | 7.82 |
| Chatterbox original | 47.0 s | 3.40 | 0.64 s | ok | 0 | 7.33 |
| ×1.05 | 45.0 s | 3.55 | 0.62 s | ok | 0 | 7.21 |
| ×1.10 | 43.1 s | 3.72 | 0.60 s | ok | 0 | 7.05 |
| ×1.15 | 41.4 s | 3.89 | 0.59 s | ok | 0 | **6.75** |
| Pauses tightened | 43.9 s | 3.41 | 0.42 s | ok | 0 | 7.25 |
| Pauses + ×1.05 | 42.0 s | 3.56 | 0.41 s | ok\* | 0 | 7.06 |
| Pauses + ×1.10 | 40.2 s | 3.71 | 0.40 s | ok | 0 | 6.97 |

How the variants were made and checked:

- **"Pauses tightened"** keeps every take's audible decay plus a 120 ms guard, and removes only the near-silence (below −60 dB) after it, 2.8 s in total. Starts, comma pauses and explicit `[pause:]` marks are untouched. The style's own 0.34 s gap then applies.
- **Speed** uses the existing pitch-preserving stretch, multiplied on top of the style's 1.04×.
- **"Words"** is the offline word check on the whole narration. "rizz" is unknown to Whisper in every version, including Qwen3's. \*One run also missed "beneath"; it is the same audio stretched, so this is most likely recognition noise. Listen to section 7.
- **"Cut endings"** uses the new rule. Neither processing step introduced any.
- **"Periodicity"** is a coarse smoothness proxy. It drops steadily with stretch (−0.12 at ×1.05, −0.28 at ×1.10, −0.58 at ×1.15). That is a measurable cost; whether it is audible is for you to hear.
- **Pitch and brightness** do not change across any Chatterbox variant (166–172 Hz, 843–869 Hz). Chatterbox stays clearly brighter than Qwen3 (≈ 860 Hz against 729).

## What is technically justified

1. **Tightening Chatterbox's dead air at section ends.** Strongly justified: it removes 2.8 s of near-silence without touching speech, decay or explicit pauses, and gaps come out at about 0.42 s, between Qwen3's 0.36 s and the previous 0.64 s.
2. **A small extra speed (×1.05, possibly ×1.10).** Justified only if you hear no loss. The cost grows with speed, and ×1.15 has the largest measured texture change.
3. **Comma pauses inside sentences:** left alone. They are probably part of Chatterbox's naturalness.
4. **`cfg_weight`:** a possible generation-level way to change Chatterbox's native pace, but it doubles generation time and is unmeasured. It would need a small, safe generation test with your approval.

**Starting recommendation for your listening:** pauses tightened + ×1.05 (42.0 s), against pauses tightened alone (43.9 s) and + ×1.10 (40.2 s).

## Stage B — implemented (25 October 2026)

You chose **pauses tightened + ×1.10 (40.2 s)**. It is now Chatterbox's Energetic Explainer default.

**What changed**

- **`voice_studio/styles.py` `ENGINE_TUNING`**: one entry, `('chatterbox', ENERGETIC): speed_factor 1.10, dead_air_guard_s 0.12`. A new Chatterbox Energetic take is stretched to 1.04 × 1.10 = **1.144×**. The take records both values (`engine_speed_factor`, `dead_air_guard_s`).
- **`voice_studio/audio.py` `join_segments`**: for a take carrying `dead_air_guard_s`, assembly keeps everything up to the end of the audible decay (last 10 ms block above −60 dBFS) plus 0.12 s. Only the near-silence after that is dropped. The style gap is then computed from the quiet that is actually kept.
- **No new controls.** The setting is internal, as agreed.

**What did not change**

- Qwen3 in any style, Natural for both engines, comma pauses, starts of sections.
- Explicit `[pause:]` marks, which still insert exactly the requested silence.
- Manual engine selection, one-click generation, resource admission and monitoring.
- **Existing takes.** A take made before this change has no tuning recorded, so it is neither regenerated, re-timed nor trimmed, and assembles exactly as before. Only takes generated from now on use the new pacing.

**Checked against your choice.** Rebuilding the `c40802da` narration from its saved raw takes through the app's own path gives **40.15 s**, against 40.21 s for the variant you picked. Gaps between sections match to within a few hundredths of a second. The small remainder comes from trimming after the tempo stretch instead of before it. Every audible decay sample is kept bit for bit, and no section ends cut.

**One fault found and fixed on the way.** The first build came out at 40.85 s. Faint peaks in the discarded dead air made assembly think the take kept no quiet, so it added the full 0.34 s gap on top of the 0.12 s guard. Assembly now counts only the kept audio. A regression test covers this.

**To undo:** empty `ENGINE_TUNING` in `voice_studio/styles.py` (or delete the one entry). New takes then generate and assemble exactly as before. Takes made while the tuning was on keep their recorded speed and trim until they are regenerated.

**Tests:** 254 pass, including 10 in `tests/test_chatterbox_pacing.py`. The GUI checks pass.
