Which engine narrates better?

Same script, same voice (Mike), level-matched. Your ears decide this one.

Every clip is matched to the same loudness (-20 LUFS) with a single constant gain. Nothing else was altered — no compression, no pitch or timing change. The original recordings are untouched in samples/engines/.

The measurements said Chatterbox is 6.5× slower (a 45-second narration takes about 7 minutes instead of 31 seconds) and that its expressiveness control did not behave consistently. Measurements cannot judge naturalness, so ignore all of that until you have listened.

What to listen for

  • Naturalness — does it sound like a person explaining, or a machine reading?
  • Emotional expression — is there genuine interest, or flat recitation?
  • Intonation — does pitch move with the meaning? Does the question rise?
  • Emphasis — in sample 4, does “absolutely no idea” stand out?
  • Sentence endings — does “The mystery is over” feel finished, or cut off?
  • Pacing — are the pauses where a person would put them?
  • Voice consistency — does it stay recognisably Mike throughout?
  • Clarity — any slurred, dropped or mispronounced words?
